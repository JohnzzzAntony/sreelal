My Process

I combine user research, clear structure and careful visual craft to build interfaces people find easy to use.

01. Discovery & Research

Research Understanding users & goals

I start by understanding the product, its users and the business objectives — through stakeholder conversations, competitor analysis and user journey mapping.

02. Wireframes & User Flows

Structure Mapping the journey

Information architecture, user flows and low-fidelity wireframes establish the structure before any visual decisions are made.

03. UI Design & Prototyping

Design High-fidelity interfaces

High-fidelity screens and interactive prototypes in Figma and Adobe XD, built on a consistent design system for web and mobile.

04. Handoff & Design QA

Delivery Pixel-perfect implementation

Developer handoff with specs and assets, followed by visual QA so the build matches the design accurately across breakpoints.
